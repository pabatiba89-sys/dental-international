FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find Answers to the Questions You Care About Most

How long do I need to stay in Huangshan?
Confirm the appointment sequence before arranging a stay. Assessment, preparatory care, healing and the final restoration may involve separate visits. Your dentist should explain the timetable for your own plan.
Can I get all my treatment done in one visit?
One appointment is not appropriate for every treatment. Ask which procedures may be combined, whether any teeth provided are temporary and which stages require healing or a return visit.
Can patients with diabetes or high blood pressure get dental implants?
Medical conditions and medicines need an individual assessment. Tell the dental team about your health and ask whether coordination with your usual medical clinician is needed before treatment.
How painful is dental implant surgery?
Discuss pain control and anxiety before implant surgery. Your team should explain the anaesthesia options and provide personalised recovery instructions and a contact number for concerns.
Can I see the final result before treatment?
A digital design or mock-up can help discuss shape and appearance when appropriate. It is a planning aid and does not guarantee a particular clinical result.
Do I need a visa to visit China for dental treatment?
Entry requirements depend on nationality and can change. Check current official immigration guidance and the requirements for your itinerary before booking travel.
What is the nearest airport for international travellers?
The right arrival airport depends on the confirmed clinic address and your itinerary. Ask the clinic to confirm a suitable route and any transfers before buying tickets.
Do you provide accommodation and transportation support?
Transport and accommodation are not confirmed services in this preview. Ask the operating clinic for written arrangements, inclusions, costs and a contact for schedule changes.
Can I travel around Huangshan during my dental treatment?
Discuss activity and travel plans with your treating dentist. Recovery needs and review appointments should determine when sightseeing is appropriate.
How much does dental treatment cost at Dental International?
The displayed figures are sample prices for design review. A real estimate must be based on an examination and should identify the procedures, materials, visits and follow-up included.
Are there any hidden fees?
Ask for an itemised estimate, including possible additional procedures, temporary and final restorations, reviews and repair arrangements. Agree on how changes to the estimate will be communicated.
Do you provide follow-up support after I return home?
Confirm the follow-up plan and how to contact the treating team before leaving. Obtain a treatment summary for your usual dentist and discuss arrangements for problems after returning home.
